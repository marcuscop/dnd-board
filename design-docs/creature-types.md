Aberration: Utterly alien beings with strange anatomies and psychic powers.

Beast: Natural, non-historical animals, giant variants, and dinosaurs.

Celestial: Holy beings native to the Upper Planes.

Construct: Animated objects, golems, or manufactured automata infused with magic .

Dragon: Reptilian scaled creatures of ancient power.

Elemental: Beings composed of the primal elements of air, earth, fire, or water.

Fey: Magical creatures strongly tied to nature and the Feywild.

Fiend: Wicked creatures native to the Lower Planes.

Giant: Towering, intelligent humanoids with a strict social hierarchy.

Humanoid: Bipedal peoples of civilized societies and cultures, including standard player species and NPC mortals

Monstrosity: Frightening, unnatural monsters that do not fit neatly into other categories, often born of magic or curses.

Ooze: Amorphous, gelatinous slime or jelly lacking traditional anatomy.

Plant: Ambulatory or magical vegetable and fungal life forms.

Undead: Once-living creatures reanimated by necromancy or dark curses.
