## Martial Archetypes

Fighter subclasses are called Martial Archetypes. At 3rd level, you choose an archetype that you strive to emulate in your combat styles and techniques. The archetype you choose grants you features at 3rd level and again at 7th, 10th, 15th, and 18th level. Here are the archetypes and their links that contain their descriptions
