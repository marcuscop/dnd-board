# Half Cover

Coverage: An obstacle blocks at least half of the target's body (such as low walls, furniture, or another creature).

Benefit: Grants a +2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws.

# Three-Quarters Cover

Coverage: An obstacle blocks about three-quarters of the target's body (such as thick pillars, portcullises, or arrow slits).

Benefit: Grants a +5 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws. 

# Full Cover

Benefit: The creature cannot be targeted directly by attacks or spells, however only AOE spells that explicitly state that they spread around corners (like Fireball), it damages normally.
