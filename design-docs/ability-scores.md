## Your Dungeon Master (DM) decides which method your group uses:

### Standard Array
Assign this exact set of six fixed numbers to your stats: 15, 14, 13, 12, 10, and 8.

### Point Cost (Point Buy)
You start with 27 points to spend on your stats. Each score has a specific point cost (for example, a score of 8 costs 0 points, a 14 costs 7 points, and a 15 costs 9 points). No base score can exceed 15 before bonuses.

####  Point Cost Table

#### Important Rules to Remember
The Minimum and Maximum limits: You cannot buy a score lower than 8, and you cannot buy a base score higher than 15 before applying your background bonuses.
The Price Jump: Notice that jumping from a score of 13 to 14 costs 2 points, and moving from 14 to 15 costs another 2 points. Every other step only costs 1 point.
The Standard Array Connection: The Standard Array preset (15, 14, 13, 12, 10, 8) uses exactly 27 points, making it one of many valid combinations you can build using this system.

### Random Generation (Rolling)
Roll four six-sided dice (4d6), drop the lowest single die, and add the remaining three numbers together. Repeat this process six total times to get your six scores, then assign them to your abilities.
